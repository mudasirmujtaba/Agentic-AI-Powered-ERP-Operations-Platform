# Order Fulfilment Procedure

Owner: Sales Operations.

## Order lifecycle

Sales orders move through Draft, Confirmed, Processing, Shipped and Delivered. Draft orders can be edited freely.
Confirming an order checks the customer's credit and reserves stock in the fulfilment warehouse; if stock is short
the order cannot be confirmed until stock arrives or the quantities change.

## Service levels

Confirmed orders should start processing within one business day and ship within three business days of
confirmation. Orders past their required date are reported as late on the dashboard and reviewed daily.

## Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled until they ship. Cancelling releases any reserved stock. Shipped orders cannot be cancelled;
use a customer return instead.

## Returns

Customers may return unused goods within 30 days of delivery. Returned goods are added back to stock as a customer
return after inspection. Damaged returns are written off.

## Invoicing

Finance invoices an order once it has shipped. Each order is invoiced once.
