# Procurement Policy

Owner: Head of Operations. Applies to all purchases of stock and supplies.

## Approval thresholds

Purchase orders with a total value of $10,000 or less are approved automatically when submitted, provided the
supplier is active and every product is in the catalog.

Purchase orders above $10,000 must be approved by a Manager or Administrator before they are sent to the supplier.
Procurement users may create and submit these orders but cannot approve them.

Purchase orders above $50,000 additionally require a written justification in the order notes describing the
demand that drives the purchase.

## Supplier selection

Order from the product's primary supplier unless it is inactive or cannot meet the required date. Switching to an
alternative supplier requires a note on the purchase order explaining why.

Suppliers whose average lead time increases by more than 30% over a quarter are reviewed by procurement.

## AI-assisted recommendations

The OpsPilot Copilot may draft purchase recommendations. A recommendation is never sent to a supplier directly: an
authorised user must approve it, the resulting purchase orders are created as drafts, and the normal approval
thresholds above still apply to each draft.

## Receiving goods

Goods are received against the purchase order in OpsPilot on the day they arrive. Partial deliveries are recorded as
partial receipts; the order stays open until every line is received or the remainder is cancelled.
