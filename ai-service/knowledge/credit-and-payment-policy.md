# Credit and Payment Policy

Owner: Finance.

## Credit limits

Every customer has a credit limit. A sales order can only be confirmed if the customer's exposure — unpaid invoice
balances plus confirmed orders not yet invoiced — stays within that limit after the new order.

Customers on hold cannot have new orders confirmed. Finance places a customer on hold when any invoice is more than
60 days overdue, and releases the hold once the account is brought up to date.

Credit limit increases above $100,000 require approval from the Finance Manager.

## Payment terms

Standard payment terms are net 30 days from the invoice date. Strategic customers may be granted net 45 or net 60
terms with Finance approval. The due date is set when the invoice is issued.

## Overdue invoices

An invoice is overdue the day after its due date. Reminders are sent at 7, 14 and 30 days overdue. At 60 days
overdue the account is placed on hold.

## Payments

Payments are recorded against the invoice they settle, with the payment method and reference. Partial payments are
accepted; the invoice stays partially paid until the balance is zero.
